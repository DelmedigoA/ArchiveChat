About Bearing Witness Gaza:

Bearing Witness is an independent, grassroots, long-term testimonial initiative, established out of the urgent need to confront the ongoing humanitarian, political, and human crisis in the Gaza Strip through facts, context, and systematic, evidence-based documentation.

It is currently the only platform that consolidates and contextualizes a large body of relevant information concerning developments in Gaza, bringing together place, time, and context. The project is more than an archive: it builds a long-term knowledge infrastructure designed to enable future understanding of events as part of an ongoing historical, political, and human continuum.

Bearing Witness: Gaza cross-references firsthand testimonies, data, visual documentation, official statements, reports, and investigations from credible bodies, situating them along a clear spatial and temporal axis. This integrative approach makes it possible to identify not only discrete events, but also underlying trends unfolding in the background, such as:

Processes of dehumanization in public and political discourse
Ongoing discussions about the future of Gaza, including plans and proposals on multiple sides
The influence of US policy on the conduct of the war, international discourse, and operational boundaries
By connecting multiple layers of information, the project presents a nuanced picture that is not visible when information is dispersed, decontextualized, or presented as isolated and contradictory fragments.

This project is built on a core, research-based document entitled Bearing Witness – Gaza. Written by historian Dr. Lee Mordechai, the document was created utilizing rigorous research, critical analysis of sources, and cross-verification of evidence from wide-ranging, credible sources. The document serves as the analytical anchor and interpretive foundation of the entire Bearing Witness documentation project.
